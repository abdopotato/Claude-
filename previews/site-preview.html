FAQ

Questions committees ask us

Do you manufacture the carpet?

No. We're specialist suppliers. We source luxury prayer carpet from partner mills, import it and inspect every roll in Canada. That lets us pick the best mill for each job instead of pushing one factory's range.

How long does delivery take?

It depends on the colourway, spacing and quantity. We confirm your delivery window in writing with your quote, before you pay anything. (Add typical lead times once confirmed.)

Is installation included?

Installation is available on every order and quoted alongside the carpet, so you can compare supply-only and supply-and-install. The Jumu'ah hall package always includes a site visit and floor template.

Is the carpet fire rated?

Yes. The pile is certified to CAN/ULC-S102 and ASTM E648. We can send the certificates to your building inspector or insurer.

Can our committee pay by purchase order?

Yes. We accept POs and bank transfer, and we invoice in your mosque's registered name for your records.

Can we see the carpet first?

Yes. Request samples of any colourway through our quote form and we'll arrange them for your committee.

Installation service

Fitted by a crew that knows where the saff should fall

Carpet is only half the job. We measure your hall, plan the roll layout so seams sit between rows, and fit everything with the prayer lines squared to the qibla wall, on a schedule that works around salah.

Book a site visitTry the fit calculator

What happens on install day

1

Set out

We mark a true line off the qibla wall. Every row is measured from it, not from the room's walls.

2

Prepare

We lift the old carpet if you've added removal, then clean and check the subfloor.

3

Lay & seam

Rolls go down with seams placed between prayer rows, and the lines are matched across every join.

4

Finish & hand over

We trim the edges, fit the thresholds, vacuum and walk the hall with your committee.

Supply + install, or supply only

Every quote shows both prices. Many committees have us do the full job. Some have a trusted local installer, and that's fine too. We'll send a layout drawing showing the seam positions and saff lines for your installer to follow.

Service area: we install across Canada, and any travel is itemised in your quote.

Before we arrive: a checklist for your committee

  • Clear the hall of shelves, Qur'an stands and chairs
  • Agree install days that avoid Jumu'ah and janazah
  • Arrange access for the crew and a place to park near the door
  • Point out any heating vents, floor boxes or the minbar base
  • Let the congregation know about a temporary prayer space

A Canadian supplier of prayer-hall carpet

MosqueCarpetsCanada.ca exists for one kind of room: the prayer hall. We supply and install luxury carpet rolls for mosques, musallas and Islamic centres across Canada.

Why we don't make carpet

We're suppliers, not manufacturers, and we think that's better for you. Because we aren't tied to one factory, we can choose the mill whose pile, colour and line accuracy best suit your hall and your budget.

We import the rolls, inspect them here and install them ourselves, so the company that recommended the carpet is the one responsible for how it looks on the floor.

Why the saff matters to us

A prayer-hall carpet is a working surface. Rows that wander, or spacing that crowds sujood, make every salah a little harder. So we start from how your congregation prays, then choose the carpet.
