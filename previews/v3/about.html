سجاد المساجد

A Canadian supplier of prayer-hall carpet

MosqueCarpetsCanada.ca exists for one kind of room: the prayer hall. We supply and install luxury carpet rolls for mosques, musallas and Islamic centres across Canada.

Why we don't make carpet

We're suppliers, not manufacturers, and we think that's better for you. Because we aren't tied to one factory, we can choose the mill whose pile, colour and line accuracy best suit your hall and your budget.

We import the rolls, inspect them here and install them ourselves, so the company that recommended the carpet is the one responsible for how it looks on the floor.

Why the saff matters to us

A prayer-hall carpet is a working surface. Rows that wander, or spacing that crowds sujood, make every salah a little harder. So we start from how your congregation prays, then choose the carpet.

What we promise every committee

Straight answers

Itemised quotes that separate carpet, import and installation, with nothing hidden in "misc."

Measured before cut

Nothing is cut until your hall's dimensions are confirmed.

Respect for the space

Installs scheduled around salah, a clean site, and a walk-through with your committee at the end.

Tell us about your hall

We'll recommend a spacing and a layout at no cost.
