Installation service

Fitted by a crew that knows where the saff should fall

Carpet is only half the job. We measure your hall, plan the roll layout so seams sit between rows, and fit everything with the prayer lines squared to the qibla wall, on a schedule that works around salah.

Book a site visitTry the fit calculator

What happens on install day

1

Set out

We mark a true line off the qibla wall. Every row is measured from it, not from the room's walls.

2

Prepare

We lift the old carpet if you've added removal, then clean and check the subfloor.

3

Lay & seam

Rolls go down with seams placed between prayer rows, and the lines are matched across every join.

4

Finish & hand over

We trim the edges, fit the thresholds, vacuum and walk the hall with your committee.

Supply + install, or supply only

Every quote shows both prices. Many committees have us do the full job. Some have a trusted local installer, and that's fine too. We'll send a layout drawing showing the seam positions and saff lines for your installer to follow.

Service area: we install across Canada, and any travel is itemised in your quote.

Before we arrive: a checklist for your committee

  • Clear the hall of shelves, Qur'an stands and chairs
  • Agree install days that avoid Jumu'ah and janazah
  • Arrange access for the crew and a place to park near the door
  • Point out any heating vents, floor boxes or the minbar base
  • Let the congregation know about a temporary prayer space
