Questions committees ask us

Do you manufacture the carpet?

No. We're specialist suppliers. We source luxury prayer carpet from partner mills, import it and inspect every roll in Canada. That lets us pick the best mill for each job instead of pushing one factory's range.

How long does delivery take?

It depends on the colourway, spacing and quantity. We confirm your delivery window in writing with your quote, before you pay anything.

Is installation included?

Installation is available on every order and quoted alongside the carpet, so you can compare supply-only and supply-and-install. The Jumu'ah hall package always includes a site visit and floor template.

Is the carpet fire rated?

Yes. The pile is certified to CAN/ULC-S102 and ASTM E648. We can send the certificates to your building inspector or insurer.

Can our committee pay by purchase order?

Yes. We accept POs and bank transfer, and we invoice in your mosque's registered name for your records.

Can we see the carpet first?

Yes. Request samples of any colourway through our quote form and we'll arrange them for your committee.

Ready to re-carpet your musalla?

Send your hall dimensions or a floor plan. We'll reply with a spacing recommendation, a roll layout and an itemised quote covering supply, import and installation.
