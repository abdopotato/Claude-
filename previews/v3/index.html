How installation works

Questions committees ask us

Do you make the carpet yourselves?

We're specialist suppliers. We choose each carpet from partner mills, import it and inspect every roll in Canada. Not being tied to one factory means we pick the mill that suits your hall and budget.

How long does delivery take?

It depends on the colourway, spacing and quantity. We confirm your delivery date in writing once your measurements are confirmed, before anything is cut.

Is installation included?

Yes, if you'd like it. Every quote shows two prices, supply only and supply + installation, so your committee can compare. The Jumu'ah hall package always includes a site visit and floor template.

Will we miss Jumu'ah during the install?

No. We plan install days around salah times and Jumu'ah, and agree the dates with your committee in advance.

Do you remove the old carpet?

Yes. Removing the old carpet can be added to your quote as its own line, so your committee can see the cost.

Is the carpet fire rated?

Yes. The pile is certified to CAN/ULC-S102 and ASTM E648. We can send the certificates to your building inspector or insurer.

Can our committee pay by purchase order?

Yes. We accept POs and bank transfer, and we invoice in your mosque's registered name for your records.

Can we see the carpet first?

Yes. Request samples of any colourway through our quote form and we'll arrange them for your committee.

How do we look after the carpet?

Vacuum regularly along the direction of the pile, and blot spills straight away with a clean, damp cloth rather than rubbing. A professional deep clean once or twice a year keeps the pile and the saff lines looking sharp.

Ready to re-carpet your prayer hall?

Send your hall dimensions or a floor plan. We'll reply with a spacing recommendation, a roll layout and an itemised quote covering supply, import and installation.
