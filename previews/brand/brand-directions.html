Proposed brand identity, for your approval

Laid for the saff.

MosqueCarpetsCanada.ca is the carpet partner a mosque committee can trust with the most-used floor in the community. We don't sell "carpet"; we deliver straight rows, soft sujood and one team that answers the phone from first quote to first salah.

The idea in one line

Brand promise

Every row straight. Every roll right. One team, start to finish.

Who it's for

Masjid committees, imams and facility volunteers who must justify a large spend to their community and can't afford a carpet that crowds the saff.

Why us, not a carpet shop

We choose from many mills (we're not tied to one factory), we understand prayer-line spacing and qibla alignment, and we install it ourselves.

Reverent

Warm

We serve communities, not "customers". Friendly, patient, plain-spoken.

Dependable

Canadian, accountable, on schedule. We say what we'll do and do it.

Signature elements

The saff line

A thin brass horizontal line, used as dividers, underlines and backgrounds. It's literally what we sell: woven prayer lines.

Our frame for photos and cards. Instantly reads as "mosque" without clichés.

The khatam star

The 8-point star from Islamic geometry, as a small mark and a pattern band. Used sparingly.

Marketing and ease-of-use review of the current homepage

Shorter page, buying first, more life.

Today the homepage has 12 sections at a similar volume, mostly cream boxes on cream. Visitors have to scroll past installation details to reach colours and the calculator, which are part of choosing a carpet. My suggestions, in order of impact:

What I'd change

1. Put "choosing" together

Hall sizes → colourways → fit calculator should sit next to each other. Right now colourways and the calculator are 4 sections below the hall sizes, after installation.

2. Cut 12 sections to 9

Merge "Four steps" with "Installation" into one "How it works". Fold the line-spacing explainer into the hall-size area as a short "Which spacing?" note.

3. Shorter, braver hero

The headline runs 4 lines. Proposed: "Laid for the saff." plus one sentence, two buttons ("Shop hall sizes", "Request a quote") and a large mihrab-framed image instead of the small illustration.

4. Rhythm, not sameness

Alternate dark green, cream and one maroon band, separated by a brass saff line. Fewer outlined boxes, more full-width bands. This is what brings back the identity.

5. Proof near the money

Add a testimonial and "mosques we've carpeted" right after the hall sizes, where people decide to buy. (Needs item #17 from you.)

6. Clear navigation

Menu: Hall sizes · Colours · Installation · About · FAQ. "Fit calculator" becomes a button inside Hall sizes, not a menu item.

Two small bugs I spotted

Trust strip: the grey dome shapes above the four trust points are cut off at the top, which looks broken. Fix: show them in full or remove them.

Footer: links in each column run together on one line ("By hall size Colourways"). Fix: one link per line.

Proposed homepage order

  1. Bismillah bar + headerUnchanged, with the new logo mark if approved.
  2. Hero: "Laid for the saff."One sentence, two buttons, large arched image. Trust points sit inside the hero bottom.
  3. Shop by hall size (slider with Buy now)With a "Not sure? Use the fit calculator" link and a one-line spacing tip.
  4. ColourwaysRight after sizes, because it's the next decision.
  5. What committees sayTestimonial + mosques served (shown only once you send one).
  6. How it works: measure → choose → import → installMerges "Four steps" and "Installation" into one dark band.
  7. Sourced, not manufacturedThe sourcing story, shorter, with the rolls illustration.
  8. Fit calculatorAlso reachable from the hall-size section.
  9. FAQ, then closing quote banner and footerUnchanged content, footer links fixed.

The line-spacing explainer (90 / 95 / 100cm) moves into the product page and a short tip under the hall sizes. Nothing is deleted; it's re-ordered and merged.
