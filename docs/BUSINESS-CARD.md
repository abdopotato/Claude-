# Business card: MosqueCarpetsCanada.ca

Everything you need to design and print the card. **Known** items come from the website and the store; anything
marked ✏️ is still blank and needs your details before printing.

## 1. What goes on the card

### Front (brand side)

| Item | What to use |
|---|---|
| Business name | **MosqueCarpetsCanada.ca** (".ca" in gold, as on the website header) |
| Arabic name | **سجاد المساجد** (Mosque Carpets), under the name, as on the website |
| Logo | The mosque mark from the website header (white on dark green). A new arch logo was proposed but not yet approved. |
| Tagline (optional, pick one) | "Luxury prayer-hall carpet, supplied and installed" (safe, matches the site) · "Laid for the saff." · "Every row, straight." · "From the mill to the mihrab." |

### Back (contact side)

| Item | What to use |
|---|---|
| Your name | ✏️ |
| Title | ✏️ e.g. Owner / Founder / Installation Lead |
| Phone | ✏️ business number, e.g. +1 (416) 555-0123 |
| WhatsApp | ✏️ same as phone, or a separate number (add a small WhatsApp icon if it is) |
| Email | ✏️ **a business address**, e.g. info@mosquecarpetscanada.ca. Don't print the personal Gmail the store uses now. |
| Website | **mosquecarpetscanada.ca** (no "https://" or "www") |
| Service area | "Serving mosques across Canada" (matches the site). Add your city if you'd like, e.g. "Based in ✏️, Ontario". |
| What you do (one line) | "Prayer-hall carpet · Supply & installation" |
| QR code (recommended) | Links to the **Request a quote** page: `mosquecarpetscanada.ca/pages/contact` |
| Arabic closing (optional) | **جزاكم الله خيرا** (Jazakum Allahu khayran), as in the website footer |

Keep it to these lines. Prices, fire ratings and spacing details belong on the website, not the card.

## 2. Check before you print

- [ ] **The .ca address works.** The store currently runs on `mosquecarpetscanada.myshopify.com`, and
      **mosquecarpetscanada.ca is not connected to it yet** (Shopify → Settings → Domains). Connect it, then open
      `mosquecarpetscanada.ca` on your phone to confirm the site loads before printing it on cards.
- [ ] **The business email works.** It needs the .ca domain and an email service, e.g. Google Workspace or
      Shopify Email forwarding. Send yourself a test email.
- [ ] **The QR code works.** Scan it with two different phones; it should open the quote form.
- [ ] **Phone and WhatsApp numbers** checked digit by digit.
- [ ] **Store name** changed from "My Store" to "MosqueCarpetsCanada.ca" (Settings → Store details), so
      customers who scan the card see the right name in their browser tab and on order emails.
- [ ] **Arabic text** is pasted, not retyped, and reads right-to-left in the design file. Have an Arabic reader
      check it on the final proof.

## 3. Brand colours

The same colours as the website, so the card and the site match.

| Name | Use on the card | HEX | RGB | CMYK (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Masjid Green | Main background / logo tile | #0F2822 | 15, 40, 34 | 75, 40, 60, 75 |
| Brass | Accent lines, ".ca", icons | #B8892F | 184, 137, 47 | 15, 40, 90, 15 |
| Light Brass | Gold text on the green background | #D7AE5C | 215, 174, 92 | 10, 30, 75, 0 |
| Plaster (cream) | Light background / back side | #F3EEE2 | 243, 238, 226 | 3, 4, 10, 0 |
| Maroon | Optional second accent | #5C2A2A | 92, 42, 42 | 30, 85, 70, 50 |
| Ink | Body text on cream | #1B211E | 27, 33, 30 | 70, 55, 65, 75 |

The CMYK values are starting points. Ask the printer for a hard proof, because dark green and gold shift the
most in print. For a premium look, ask about **gold foil** for the brass parts instead of printed gold ink.

## 4. Fonts (both free, from Google Fonts)

- **Amiri**: business name, your name, and all Arabic text. It sets English and Arabic beautifully together.
- **Work Sans**: contact details (phone, email, website). It stays clear at small sizes.
- Minimum sizes: contact details 7–8 pt; your name 10–12 pt; business name 12–14 pt.

## 5. Print specifications (standard Canadian card)

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Size | 3.5 × 2 in (88.9 × 50.8 mm) |
| Bleed | 0.125 in (3 mm) on every side, so the file is 3.75 × 2.25 in |
| Safe zone | Keep text and the QR code 0.125 in (3 mm) inside the trim line |
| Resolution | 300 dpi for any images |
| Colour mode | CMYK |
| File | Print-ready PDF (PDF/X-1a if the printer asks), fonts embedded or outlined |
| Paper | 16 pt or thicker, matte or soft-touch; optional gold foil on the logo and ".ca" |
| QR code | At least 0.75 in (19 mm) square, dark on light, with a quiet margin around it |

## 6. Suggested layout

**Front:** Masjid Green background · logo mark centred · "MosqueCarpetsCanada**.ca**" in Light Brass Amiri ·
**سجاد المساجد** underneath · optional thin brass line (a "saff line") across the bottom.

**Back:** Plaster background · your name and title top-left · phone, WhatsApp, email and website stacked
underneath with small brass icons · QR code on the right with "Scan for a free quote" (only say "free" if quotes
stay free; the site currently says "Free and itemised, with no obligation") · **جزاكم الله خيرا** small at the bottom.
