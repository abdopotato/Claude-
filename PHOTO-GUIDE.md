# Photo guide: product and site images

Consistent photos are the fastest way to make the store look professional. Until photos exist,
the site shows matching drawings of each hall size, so every card keeps the same shape.

## Shot list (per hall size / colourway)

| # | Shot | Why it matters | Used on |
|---|------|----------------|---------|
| 1 | **Hero:** the full carpet installed in a real prayer hall, shot from the back of the hall toward the qibla wall, at standing eye height | Shows the saff lines running true, which is the main selling point | Product page (first image), hall-size card |
| 2 | **Pile close-up:** a hand or coin on the pile for scale, raking light from the side | Shows luxury and density | Product page |
| 3 | **Saff line detail:** one woven line, straight down from above | Proves the lines are woven, not printed | Product page |
| 4 | **Seam:** a finished seam between rows | Answers "will we see the join?" | Product page (mid-size and large) |
| 5 | **Roll edge:** the rolled carpet end-on, showing the backing | Quality signal for committees | Product page |
| 6 | **Colourway swatch:** a flat 30 × 30 cm sample, straight down from above, daylight | Colours must match across the site | Colourways section |
| 7 | **Warehouse / inspection:** rolls on racks, or someone checking a roll | Supports "sourced, inspected in Canada" | Sourcing section |
| 8 | **Install team at work:** hands, tools, the chalk line off the qibla wall | Supports the installation service | Installation page |

## Technical rules

- **Size:** at least 2048 × 2048 px. Upload the original and Shopify makes smaller copies automatically.
- **Shape:** product photos **square (1:1)**. Hall-card and sourcing photos **landscape (16:9 or 5:4)**. Keep the subject centred, because the site crops to these shapes.
- **Light:** daylight or neutral white light, with no coloured mosque lighting on colourway shots. The same white balance across a product's photos.
- **Background:** real halls for installed shots; plain cream or light grey for close-ups.
- **No stock photos.** Buyers can tell, and it undercuts the "we source it, we check it" message.
- **No people's faces** in prayer without their permission. Shoot empty halls or hands and tools.
- **Format:** JPG or PNG from the camera is fine; Shopify serves modern formats automatically.

## File names and alt text

- Name files so they sort together: `main-hall-8x12_masjid-green_01-hero.jpg`.
- Alt text describes what's in the photo for people using screen readers, and helps search:
  - Good: "Masjid Green prayer carpet installed in an 8 by 12 metre hall, saff lines running toward the qibla wall"
  - Bad: "carpet", "IMG_4032", or a list of keywords

## Getting them onto the site

Send a shared folder or public links. Each image needs a public web address for me to attach it to
a product (Shopify's rule for this connection). Or upload them yourself in Shopify admin,
**Products → [product] → Media**, and I'll set the order and alt text.
