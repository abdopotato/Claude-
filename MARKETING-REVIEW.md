# Marketing review of the live store (2026-10-03)

Reviewed: live theme "Site update v7 (layout)" (MAIN), the 3 products, and the Installation, About and Quote pages.
The live theme files match `theme/`. Nothing on the store was changed. Every rewrite below needs owner approval
before it's applied (see `CHANGE-PLAN.md`).

## What works

1. **A sharp niche.** The site speaks to mosque committees in their own words (saff, qibla, sujood, Jumu'ah,
   janazah). Generic carpet sellers can't copy that, and it's the brand's biggest asset.
2. **Prices are public.** Most commercial flooring sellers hide them. "From $3,650 CAD" lets a committee budget
   before calling anyone.
3. **The FAQ answers committee questions:** purchase orders, invoicing in the mosque's name, fire certificates
   for the inspector. Those are the questions a board actually asks.
4. **Low-risk promises:** "nothing is cut until we confirm your measurements" and "itemised quote".
5. **Supplier, not manufacturer, turned into a benefit:** "we're never tied to one factory's range."
6. **Installation page:** the committee checklist ("avoid Jumu'ah and janazah", "temporary prayer space") is
   the most helpful content on the site.
7. **The quote form is short:** only name and email are required, and it pre-fills size, spacing and colour
   from the page the visitor came from.
8. **Look and feel:** green, brass and cream, the bismillah bar and Arabic labels feel dignified and fit the setting.

## What I'd change, in order of impact

### 1. Trust: the site claims "luxury" but doesn't show it yet
For a $3,650–$9,950 purchase approved by a committee, trust decides the sale. At the moment:
- there are no photos (every image is a drawing),
- there are no reviews or testimonials and no projects you've done,
- nobody is named, the About page has no person on it,
- the store is still called **"My Store"** (shows in Google, browser tabs and order emails),
- the primary domain is **mosquecarpetscanada.myshopify.com**, not the .ca the brand is named after,
- the contact email is a personal Gmail and there's no phone or WhatsApp.

**Your installer background is the strongest proof you have, and the site doesn't mention it.** Online sellers
don't install, and local installers don't source specialist mosque carpet; you do both. Put your years of
installing and the number of halls or floors you've fitted on the homepage, and use photos from past
installation jobs (even phone photos of commercial carpet you laid are better than drawings).

### 2. Claims that aren't true yet
The store has **0 orders**, but it says "Most ordered" (Main hall card), "Our most-ordered shade" (Masjid
Green) and "the configuration mosques order most" (product description). Unless these come from your
installation jobs, change them to "Recommended" or "Our pick for most halls". Also confirm or soften
"Book a **free** site visit" (free anywhere in Canada?), the fire certification and PO/bank transfer
(already in `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`).

### 3. Pricing and installation conflict in three places
- On the product page, **"Supply + professional installation" is selected by default and the price doesn't
  change.** A buyer can reasonably think installation is included, pay $3,650 and then be surprised. Make
  "Supply only" the default, or say next to the option: "Installation is quoted after we measure. You aren't
  charged for it today."
- The fit calculator says "$34–$44 per m² **including** a fitted install", but the 8 × 12 m product is $3,650
  for carpet only ($38/m²). Visitors will compare these.
- The FAQ says "we confirm your delivery window… **before you pay anything**", but "Buy now" takes full
  payment online.
- Row counts don't match: the card says ≈10 rows for 8 × 12 m, but the calculator shows 8 for the same hall
  (`OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` #4).

### 4. Decide the main action
Committees rarely pay $10k with a "Buy now" button; they ask for a quote, show it to the board and then pay
by invoice. Right now one view has a gold "Request a quote" in the header, a gold "Shop hall sizes" in the
hero and a gold "Buy now" on the card. Suggested test: keep "Buy now" on the two smaller sizes, make
"Get a quote / site visit" the main action on the Jumu'ah size, and see which brings in more leads.

### 5. Layout
- **Carousel:** buyers comparing three price tiers want to see them side by side. Hiding two cards at 30%
  opacity makes them hard to compare and hard to read. Suggestion: 3 cards side by side on desktop, carousel
  only on phones.
- **Header:** the quote button sits right after the logo. People expect the main button on the right, next to
  the cart.
- **Homepage is now very short:** hero → sizes → colours → FAQ. Bring back a compact 3-step "How it works"
  (Measure → Quote → Install) so committees can see the process, and a testimonial slot once you have one.
- **Hall sizes:** real halls are rarely 4 × 6, 8 × 12 or 15 × 22. Add "Fits halls up to ~X m²" or an
  approximate price per m² on each card, so someone with a 10 × 14 m hall knows what to do.
- **Colour swatches** are flat colour blocks. Close-up photos of the pile and the woven line sell texture,
  and texture is what makes carpet look luxurious.

### 6. Missing conversion tools (from cheapest to most work)
- **WhatsApp button** (click to chat). Many mosque committees work on WhatsApp, and it lets visitors send
  photos of their hall, which the quote form can't do.
- **A reply-time promise** on the quote form: "We reply within 1 business day."
- **A free sample kit** as the soft offer: "Free colour samples mailed to your committee." It's the easiest
  yes and gets you a contact for follow-up.
- **A committee pack:** one PDF with the specs, fire certificates, photos and a sample quote, which the
  person who found you can forward to the board.
